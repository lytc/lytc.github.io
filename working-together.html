Working together

I’m looking for an engineering role focused on architecture, platforms, and better development workflows.

Based in Vietnam. Open to remote work or a role in Ho Chi Minh City. I’m not looking to relocate internationally.

I may not be the right fit if…

  • Meetings are central to everyday work. I’m looking for a nearly meeting-free way of collaborating.
  • Technical decisions are imposed by rank. I expect different opinions to get a fair hearing and be supported by reasoning and evidence.
  • The role is mainly people management. I want to stay on the engineering track, while contributing technical guidance and support for colleagues.

Where I do my best work

  • Architecture and platforms. Shared services, reusable packages, and tools that help the whole team build software.
  • People and coding agents working together. Less time passing around context and chasing updates. work-orchestrator is what I use today; Dika is the idea I want to bring to a team.
  • Very few meetings. I see little need for meetings to exchange information. I prefer written communication and time to focus.
  • Equal voice and shared responsibility. Everyone should be able to question a decision and be responsible for the result.

How I want the team to decide

Seek agreement first. Explain different opinions with a convincing argument and evidence. If disagreement remains, decide by majority.
